What the evidence does not establish

  • Events preceding moves and measured correlations do not prove causes.
  • GDELT is machine coded and the six-month sample excludes many events and instruments.
  • A local language model can write an irrelevant but syntactically valid query. The visible Cypher and paths matter.
  • Several historic PRs lack the required review comments; the team must finish rehearsal after the stage-five merge.
